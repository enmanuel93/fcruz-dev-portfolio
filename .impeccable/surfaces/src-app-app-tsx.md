# Surface brief: portfolio home (single page)

Scope: the whole single-page portfolio (`src/app/App.tsx` and every section). Mode: Experience; the work and the career evidence lead.

Audience and job: recruiters and tech leads in Europe judging a .NET / Full Stack candidate in under a minute. Actions: download the CV (navbar) or send a message (contact form). Proof: four real projects with screenshots, four roles, nineteen technologies, 10,000+ users on the registry platform.

Constraints: preserve all content, sections, links, the CV download and the contact form; brand renamed to FCruz-Dev; profile photo unchanged; English copy; dark-only (no theme toggle); no invented stars, forks or metrics.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Pinned by the user's reference image: a dark, violet-lit developer portfolio where evidence (projects, roles, stack) carries the page. It refuses the incumbent template look: teal glass everywhere, uppercase eyebrows and serif-italic slogans.

OWN-WORLD: Near-black ground with a blue-violet cast; layered ink panels with 1px violet-tinted hairlines; one violet action colour for buttons, the headline accent, timeline nodes and focus rings; soft violet light only behind the portrait and the contact panel; Geist (self-hosted, variable) for display and text; rounded-xl corners; brand logos in their own colours on dark tiles.

STORY: The visitor reads role and stack in the first viewport, sees real projects, scans the career timeline and stack grid, then downloads the CV or writes a message.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left: role badge, a 3-line bold headline whose last phrase is violet, the bio, "View Projects" (primary) and "Contact Me" (outline) buttons, social icons. Right: the portrait in a tall violet-edged frame on a dot field, with "Available for work" and "+7 years" badges overlapping its edges. Navbar: </> FCruz-Dev logo, section links, "Download CV" primary button.

FORM: pinned by the user's reference design (not rolled); seed: none (unavailable)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
